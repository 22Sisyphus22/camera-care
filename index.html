Meet Body Guard

A personal safety and security system built for elderly and disabled people living independently. It combines SOS buttons, a wearable pendant and a bedside button with a security camera, so every alert comes with instant visual confirmation of what's happening.

Add more cameras and SOS buttons whenever you need them, and build a safety net that fits the home.

How it works

  1. Press any SOS button

    The pendant, the wall button or the bedside button. Wherever they are in the house or garden, help is within reach.

  2. Your phone is alerted

    Body Guard sends an instant alert to the app and automatically dials the contacts you've chosen.

  3. See and speak to them

    Check the camera to see what's happened, and talk to them through the unit.

Questions families ask

Can't see your question? Ask us directly.

Who is Camera Care for?

Families who want an elderly or disabled loved one to stay independent in their own home for longer, knowing that help is there the moment it's needed, wherever you are.

Which product do I need?

There are three routes: home-based safety with Body Guard, anywhere safety with an SOS smart watch, or both together. Everything connects to an app on your phone, so they know you'll be there, and you know if they need you.

How much does it cost?

The Body Guard kit is £129.99 as a one-off payment. The smart watches start at £79.00; because they work on 4G, they need a SIM card with a monthly plan from a mobile network provider.

Are there monthly fees for Body Guard?

No. Body Guard connects to the home Wi-Fi, so once you've bought the kit there's nothing more to pay.

What Wi-Fi does Body Guard need?

Body Guard works on 2.4GHz Wi-Fi. Most home routers broadcast on 2.4GHz; if yours combines bands under one name, it will usually still connect.

How far do the SOS buttons reach?

Up to 200 metres, which covers the whole house and most gardens.

Can I add more cameras and buttons later?

Yes. Extra cameras, SOS buttons, pendants and bedside buttons can be added at any time. See accessories.

Is there a waterproof button for the bathroom?

A waterproof SOS button and pendant is coming soon at £16.99. Ask to be notified when it's available.
